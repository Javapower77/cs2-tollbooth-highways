# Tollbooths Collection for CS:2

## Description

This asset pack add highways tollbooths to collect money.
There are 5 automatic and 5 manual tollbooth assets.
You can build any combination you want, and in the future I will create assets where they are combined in one prefab pack.
You can configure all cost for each type of tollbooth in the option menu of the mod.

## Added Assets
* **Toll-Automatic-HeavyTransit**: Automatic toolbooth for heavy transit vehicles. Vechicles will slow down to pay the toll.
* **Toll-Manual-HeavyTransit**: Manual toolbooth for heavy transit vehicles. Vechicles will stop to pay the toll.
* **Toll-Automatic-PrivateTransit**: Automatic toolbooth for private transit vehicles. Vechicles will slow down to pay the toll.
* **Toll-Manual-PrivateTransit**: Manual toolbooth for private transit vehicles. Vechicles will stop to pay the toll.
* **Toll-Automatic-PublicTransit**: Automatic toolbooth for public transit vehicles. Vechicles will slow down to pay the toll.
* **Toll-Manual-PublicTransit**: Manual toolbooth for public transit vehicles. Vechicles will stop to pay the toll.
* **Toll-Automatic-ServiceTransit**: Automatic toolbooth for service transit vehicles. Vechicles will slow down to pay the toll.
* **Toll-Manual-ServiceTransit**: Manual toolbooth for service transit vehicles. Vechicles will stop to pay the toll.
* **Toll-Automatic-AnyTransit**: Automatic toolbooth for any transit vehicles. Vechicles will slow down to pay the toll.
* **Toll-Manual-AnyTransit**: Manual toolbooth for any transit vehicles. Vechicles will stop to pay the toll.

## NOTICE ON THE USAGE

When click on the tollbooth in the road there will be a popup with some information about the type, quantity and even how much money had been collected with the transit going through it.

## CAVEATS AND LIMITATIONS

I couldn't make that vehicles go through the specific tollbooth yet. I had tried many ways, using Harmony Patch, Reflection, etc. but I couldn't find a way to do it.
So, for now, all tollbooths will charge all vehicles, but you can configure the cost for each type of vehicle to 0 if you want that specific vehicle type don't pay anything.
For Public Transit tollbooths, it works because vanilla game have that feature implemented through the "Public Transport Only" road option or the CarLane.PublicOnly flag.
There is no way to impact in the Cost to avoid wrong tollbooths either.
